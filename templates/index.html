GeoSage

Intelligent Land Monitoring, Boundary Verification and Change Detection Using AI — compare old and recent satellite or drone imagery of rural land parcels, screen possible boundary mismatches, and generate explainable, human-reviewable reports. Fully offline, free and open source.

Start Analysis
Important: all AI findings produced by GeoSage are preliminary screening aids. They are never legally verified property boundaries and must be confirmed by authorized land surveyors or government officials.
WHAT GEOSAGE DOES
Change Detection

ORB feature matching + homography alignment, then image differencing, morphological filtering and contour analysis find where the land visually changed.

Boundary Verification

Upload GeoJSON/KML boundaries or draw polygons, then compare recorded vs candidate geometry (IoU, overlap, displacement) with Shapely — metric areas only with a valid CRS.

Change Heatmap

Green → yellow → red overlay shows pixel-change intensity, with adjustable opacity, region inspection and statistics. Intensity ≠ encroachment risk.

Land-Use Monitoring

A clearly-labelled classical computer-vision baseline estimates vegetation, agriculture, built-up, bare-soil and water proportions and their shifts over time.

Explainable AI

Every flagged region answers "WHY WAS THIS AREA FLAGGED?" using real processing outputs — never fabricated measurements.

Human Verification

Reviewers mark findings as pending, verified or rejected, with a complete audit trail. AI never has the final word.
